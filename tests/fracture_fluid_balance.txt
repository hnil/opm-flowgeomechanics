Accepted-step fracture fluid balance diagnostic

Enable fractureparam.solver.write_fluid_balance=true in a copy of the selected
configuration. It is off by default. The VEM problem writes one CSV per local
active fracture and MPI rank, in the simulation output directory. No output is
emitted for rejected nonlinear trials: the adaptive timestepper calls
problem.endTimeStep only in its accepted branch (which can also accept an
unconverged solution if explicitly configured). The completion log and inner
convergence columns must therefore still be checked.

Phases:
- flow_converged: at entry to the accepted endTimeStep hook, before its extra
  mechanics/fracture solve. The label denotes the call site; inspect convergence
  flags rather than assuming the optional accept-unconverged mode was disabled.
- accepted_end: after that extra solve, well-state assignment, parent endTimeStep,
  filter-cake update and optional output. No physical solve is added by this
  diagnostic. start_s and end_s explicitly identify the accepted time interval;
  native fracture PVD labels still follow their existing time convention.

Sign convention: well inflow is positive into the fracture; leakoff is positive
out to the reservoir. Rates are reservoir-volume m3/s in the audited legacy
volumetric representation. Integrated fluxes use backward-Euler end-state rates.
The diagnostic isolates the production well pressure operator and calls the
same well-source routine used by addSource. It does not substitute the well
rate target, original-perforation input rate or WWIRFRAC for fracture inflow.
LeakOfRate is multiplied by each triangle's area before summation.

Two storage rates and defects are reported:
  physical storage = (current total volume - checkpoint total volume) / dt
  model storage = (current volume - old volume represented on current mesh)
                  / fracture_dt, when the aperture storage term is enabled
  physical defect = Q_well - Q_leakoff - physical storage
  equation defect = Q_well - Q_leakoff - model storage.
Their difference reveals missing old-domain volume or a disabled storage term.
The cached assembled pressure-row residual is also summed independently, with
its cell infinity norm and configured flow tolerance. Subtracting large pressure
matrix contributions has roundoff, so report the actual cross-check difference
and compare it to the existing solver tolerance; no new conservation acceptance
band is introduced. The model's approximate Newton policy is unchanged.

The diagnostic flags applicability for the legacy coupled RegularTrimesh mode.
External/embedded pressure or optional density/viscosity-dependent flux modes
are not certified by this audit. The input well reservoir-water rate and input
original-perforation total reservoir-volume rate are context, not independently
measured fracture injection; the former can be damped by native coupling.
WWIRFRAC uses the reservoir well model's CTF split of surface water rates
(WellFractureFlowSplit.hpp), a different observable. This local fracture budget
does not alone establish conservation between that reservoir booking and the
fracture's rate-well stand-in.

Analyze with:
  python3 scripts/fracture_fluid_balance.py --run-dir RUN --log RUN.log
      --ranks 4 --report-dir NEW_ANALYSIS_DIRECTORY

The analyzer requires completed-run MPI provenance, finite values, paired phases,
unique owner/step rows, contiguous intervals and checkpoint-volume continuity.
It reports signed and absolute cumulative defects relative to BOTH delivered
fluid and aperture-volume change, so large throughputs cannot hide storage error.
It reports measurements, without automatically declaring full conservation.
The analyzer currently expects contiguous active-fracture histories; inactive
periods, restart appends or missing rows are flagged rather than filled in.
